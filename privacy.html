Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

At Stylenm, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Don't Collect

We do not collect or store:

  • Your name (it's processed only in your browser)
  • Your favorites (saved in your device's local storage)
  • Your IP address or personal details
  • Any login credentials (we have no accounts)

3. Local Storage

Your favorites list is stored in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing browser data will erase it.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We may use anonymous analytics (such as page views) to improve the website. No personally identifiable information is captured.

5. Third-Party Links

Our share buttons link to external platforms (Facebook, WhatsApp, X). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

6. Children's Privacy

Stylenm is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@stylenm.com.
