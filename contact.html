📬 What Can You Contact Us About?

Here are some common reasons people write to us:

  • Feedback & Suggestions — tell us what you love and what we can improve.
  • Bug Reports — if a style is not working or a page loads slowly, let us know.
  • New Font Requests — want a specific style or symbol added? Ask us.
  • New Category Requests — want us to add a new category like "Bollywood", "Instagram Reels", etc.? Just say so.
  • Business Enquiries — partnerships, advertising, sponsorships, and collaborations.
  • Privacy Questions — questions about your data or our privacy practices.
  • Copyright Issues — if you believe any content infringes on your rights.
  • Report Abuse — if someone is misusing our tool or services.
  • Press & Media — journalists and bloggers seeking quotes or interviews.
  • Just Saying Hi — yes, we love those too! 💌

📖 Before You Write

To help us help you faster, please check these first:

  • Read our FAQ section — most common questions are already answered there.
  • Read our Privacy Policy — for questions about your data.
  • Read our Terms & Conditions — for usage rules.

✍️ How to Write to Us

To make sure we can respond quickly and correctly, please include the following details in your email:

  • Subject line — clearly state the purpose (e.g., "Bug Report", "New Font Idea").
  • Your name — so we know who we're talking to.
  • Details — describe your question or issue in detail.
  • Screenshots — if reporting a bug, screenshots help a lot.
  • Device & browser info — for technical issues (e.g., "iPhone 13, Safari").

⏱️ Our Response Time

We are a small team, but we make sure every message gets a reply:

  • General enquiries — within 24 hours
  • Bug reports — within 48 hours
  • Business enquiries — within 2 business days
  • Legal / privacy requests — within 7 days

If you don't hear back within these timeframes, please check your spam folder — sometimes our emails end up there.
